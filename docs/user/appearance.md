# Appearance

Telar has one appearance: its default, changed however you like. Everything is in Settings → Appearance, and every change applies right away. **Restore defaults** in the header puts everything back, and the arrow beside a changed row puts back just that row.

## Theme

- **Colour scheme**: System (follows macOS), Light or Dark, picked from three previews.
- **Accent**: the colour for things you act on, like buttons, links and the caret. There are eight to choose from.
- **Depth**: how far cards, the composer and menus lift off the page.
- **Background**: None, a Gradient (four presets or your own pair of colours) or an Image (choose a file or drop one onto the row). **Strength** sets how strongly it shows. Presets have a light and a dark version; your own colours and images look the same in both schemes. Large pictures are shrunk so they can travel to every window.

## Typography

- **Interface font** and **Code font**, each with its own size and a preview underneath. Interface text runs from 13 to 18 px, and you can step it from the command palette. Pick **Custom…** to type the name of a font installed on your Mac.

## Window

- **Chat width**: how wide the conversation and the composer can grow.
- **Translucency**: lets the desktop show through the window. The desktop app on macOS only. Turning it on rebuilds the window.
- **See-through**: with translucency on, how much of the desktop shows behind the canvas and the rail.

## Where it's kept

The appearance is stored on the host, the Mac running Telar. The desktop app and every browser connected to it wear the same one, and a change made in any of them reaches the others within a few seconds. Translucency and chat width stay with the window you set them in.

The iPhone app doesn't take on the appearance.
